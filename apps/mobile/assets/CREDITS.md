# Credits

## Audio (fishing mode)

Every sound of the fishing mode comes from the **Kenney free game assets**.
Kenney's assets are released under the **CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain)**
licence, so they can be used, modified and redistributed without asking.

- Kenney — "Interface Sounds" (CC0) — `coin.mp3`, `nibble.mp3`, `correct.mp3`
- Kenney — "Sci-Fi Sounds" (CC0) — `cast.mp3`, `plop.mp3`
- Kenney — "Impact Sounds" (CC0) — `wrong.mp3`
- Kenney — "Music Jingles" (CC0) — `bite.mp3`, `frenzy.mp3`, `legend.mp3`,
  `lure.mp3`, `powerup.mp3`, `reveal.mp3`, `reel.mp3`, `snap.mp3`

Home page: <https://kenney.nl/assets>

The files are converted from the original `.ogg` to mono 22.05 kHz `.mp3` so
that they play on both Android and iOS and stay small.

## Art (placeholder)

The sprites of the fishing mode do **not** exist yet. The game draws the
placeholder shapes (a tier coloured circle with an emoji) until the PNGs from
`docs/ART-HANDOFF.md` land in `apps/mobile/assets/fishing/`. See
`apps/mobile/scripts/generate-fishing-assets.mjs`.
